Organising and Utilising MS Tools in Education

📅 08 June 2026    ⏰ 14:30 - 16:30

Responsible Use of AI in Education

📅 19 June 2026    ⏰ 14:30 - 16:30
Click to view details▼

Learn how to use AI safely, responsibly, and ethically in your classroom! Explore Microsoft’s responsible AI framework.

What you'll explore

  • Understand and articulate the core pillars of Responsible AI, including fairness, privacy, safety, transparency, inclusiveness, and accountability.
  • Equip learners with critical thinking skills to question AI outputs, detect bias, and evaluate information accuracy before accepting it.
  • Teach essential digital citizenship by guiding students on how to protect their personal information and data privacy when interacting with AI tools.
  • Foster a growth-oriented mindset where students utilise AI as a collaborative learning support and brainstorming partner rather than a shortcut for assignments.
